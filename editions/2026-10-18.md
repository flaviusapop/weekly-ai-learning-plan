# Weekly AI learning plan for Oct 18–24, about 10 hours

I based the taste check on your recent X bookmarks (personal agents, Grok Bot and Notion memory setups, harness engineering, Claude Code and Codex workflows, Lee Knowlton's notes on AI-verifiable work). Your Substack saves are all from July or earlier, so they only informed taste. Everything below is fresh long-form from roughly the last three weeks. I skipped the Luke Pierce masterclass you saved, because it is a module from his paid course for AI agency owners.

## Must Watch (3)

1. [Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_pcy1_jU27k), Dan Shipper with Willie Williams on The Every Podcast #110, about 34m, new. An honest look at why everyone's personal agents decayed and what one shared agent does better. Every launched its own agent that week, so expect a little promotion.
2. [Why every company now needs to think and operate like a lab team](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/why-every-company-now-needs-to-think), Josh Woodward (Google Labs) on Lenny's Podcast, about 1h 2m, new. How to run experiments, spot product-market fit early, and know when to kill an idea.
3. [The Science and Engineering of Agent Work](https://hugobowne.substack.com/p/the-science-and-engineering-of-agent), Hilary Mason, Eric Ma, Bryan Bischof and Tomasz Tunguz on Vanishing Gradients, about 2h 22m, new. Four heavy users show their real agent skills, memory setups and evals.

## 1) AI agents and workflows (about 1.5h more)

- [The 4-Step Agentic Engineering Workflow I Use to Ship Real Apps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rEAkEvhhcQ), Ras Mic on Behind the Craft (Peter Yang), about 47m, new. Parallel agents, validation loops, agent PR review, and the one step he never delegates.
- [nobody should type code by hand anymore. here's what's left.](https://ghuntley.com/agency/), Geoffrey Huntley, about 31m read, new. An opinionated take on vision, verification and putting the whole system under test.
- [What is Codemode](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/10/6/codemode/), Armin Ronacher, about 10m read, new. Why agents should write scripts instead of loading lots of tools into context.

## 2) Investing and trading with AI (about 2.9h)

This was the thinnest topic again. I found nothing long-form and credible on AI for discretionary order flow, and I left out the "24/7 AI trading bot" hype on X.

- [Backtest in Sierra Chart with Python (Why I Don't Use Replay Mode)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZPsoSZpaeNg), Drew Thomas at One Person Trade Desk, about 23m with the [written companion](https://onepersontradedesk.com/blog/how-to-backtest-in-sierra-chart), new. Why Sierra's fast replay modes look ahead when a strategy reads a second chart, and a Claude Code check for lookahead bias. He sells a community, but the method shown is free.
- [How He Automated His Way to 3,500 Trades a Year](https://tradingnut.com/dave-mabe/), Dave Mabe on Trading Nut #343, about 40m, new. Going from discretionary to 25 automated strategies, and how to make Claude check its own backtest code.
- [Build a Financial Analyst Harness That You Can Trust](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e9-br9hveRI), Francesco Galletta and Felippe Felisola Caso (Monzo) on the AI Engineer channel, about 1h 51m, new. A bank team builds an analyst harness step by step with verification built in.

## 3) Product and building with AI (about 1.9h more)

- [Generating Opportunity Solution Trees with AI](https://www.producttalk.org/generating-opportunity-solution-trees-with-ai-how-vistaly-rebuilt-its-product-around-interview-synthesis-evals-and-repair-loops/), Teresa Torres with the Vistaly team on Just Now Possible, about 1h 20m, new. How a discovery tool rebuilt itself around AI interview synthesis, evals and repair loops.
- [I Spent Two Years Building the Median](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JZy5AuuQzc), Hariprasad Sudharshan on the AI Engineer channel, about 36m, new. Why prompt-to-product gives you the median app, and three questions to ask before you build.
