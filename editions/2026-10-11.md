# Weekly AI learning plan for Oct 11–17, about 10 hours

I based the taste check on your recent X bookmarks (harnesses, Claude Code tips, evals, team agents, Lee Robinson and Steinberger) and your Substack saves. Most of the Substack saves are older, so they only informed taste. Everything below is fresh long-form from roughly the last three weeks, except one Must Watch you had already bookmarked.

## Must Watch (3)

1. [Claude Code's Next Era, Thariq Shihipar](https://www.latent.space/p/thariq), Latent Space, about 1h 32m, new. It covers Claude Mods, Projects/Tag, prompting as the real skill, and why harnesses age so fast.
2. [ClawLabs: Building Open Source Together](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wZYnJfL4c2Y), Peter Steinberger at OpenAI DevDay, about 35m, from your X saves. It shows what shared team agent sessions actually feel like.
3. [Where Will Investors Find Alpha in the Age of AI?](https://share.snipd.com/episode/6f9f5b83-8efc-435e-bc0b-b80276a2e5d1), Alistair Smallwood (Primer) on Invest with AI / FundamentEdge, about 1h 12m, new. It covers research agents, evals as the core skill, and where human judgment still beats autonomy.

## 1) AI agents and workflows (about 3.5h more)

- [Inside Steve Yegge's Software Factory](https://re-cinq.com/podcast/steve-yegge-software-factory), re:cinq, about 70m, new. Lessons from running about 50 agents.
- [SE Radio 740: Building Production AI Agents](https://se-radio.net/2026/09/se-radio-740-raju-dandigam-on-building-production-ai-agents/), Raju Dandigam, about 58m, new. Tool boundaries, evals and observability treated like real software.
- [How we built an agent harness that cuts token use about 90%](https://operatorsguidetoai.substack.com/p/how-we-built-an-agent-harness-that), AJ Asver, about 20m read, new. A measured cost drop from $2.89 to $0.25.

## 2) Investing and trading with AI (about 2.3h more)

This was the thinnest topic once I filtered out vendor content.
